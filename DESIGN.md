# Ops Center — Design Direction

Ops Center is a runbook control room built as a dark operations wall: amber is
reserved for the next action, green is reserved for locally checked state, and
the rest of the interface stays in gunmetal, ink, and hairline dividers.

The runbook rail, procedure sheet, focused instruction, and status panel are
one working surface rather than a dashboard of metric cards. Synthetic data is
clearly labeled, and the signature interaction is checking one procedure step
then advancing the operator's focus.
